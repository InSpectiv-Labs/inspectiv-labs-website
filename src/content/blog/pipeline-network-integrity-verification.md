Long-distance pipelines cross diverse terrain, including unstable slopes, floodplains, agricultural areas and settlement zones. Ground movement along a pipeline corridor can potentially affect buried infrastructure long before visible surface damage becomes apparent.

Monitoring an extensive network using field surveys alone can be challenging and resource-intensive.

## Our Approach

InSpectiv Labs applies satellite InSAR across the pipeline corridor to establish a regional ground-motion baseline and identify areas exhibiting persistent or changing deformation.

The analysis can highlight:
- Ground settlement along the pipeline corridor
- Slope movement affecting pipeline sections
- Subsidence and uplift zones
- Deformation near crossings and facilities
- Changes in previously stable areas

Potential hotspots are then ranked for further engineering investigation and targeted field verification.

## Outcome

Pipeline operators receive a corridor-wide view of ground behaviour, allowing inspection resources to be directed towards sections showing measurable changes rather than relying solely on periodic inspection cycles.
